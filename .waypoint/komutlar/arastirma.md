# Research before planning

Runs at stage 2 (and before a big new feature), after `sorgula` made clear what I want. `sorgula` asks what I want; this is what you need to learn to build it well.

1. If you're missing nothing for a good plan, skip this and plan.
2. Research as `rapor.md` says (reuse existing reports first, write a report for findings worth keeping, tell me in one line what you found). Choose the method and depth yourself: short for a simple project, deeper when it's complex or unclear.
3. Make technical choices yourself and record them in "Kararlar" with why. Choose the language and tools from what `sorgula` found (where it runs, speed and size, who installs it), not out of habit: compare 2–3 that fit in the report and pick the one that meets them best. Tell me only what they mean for me in plain words ("telefonda da çalışır ama ilk açılış biraz yavaş olur"). Product choices (what it does, what I see) stay mine.
